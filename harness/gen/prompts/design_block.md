# Output format
First a DESIGN block of about seven lines, then the code:

DESIGN
Theme: <one line>
Entities: <the bodies and their roles>
Mechanic twist: <what makes it original>
Actions: <each action and what it does>
Milestones: <the ordered checkpoints and what stage each marks>
Parts used: <bank parts you instantiated and their overrides, e.g. wrecking_ball(chain=250) as "wrecker"; or "none" if you built everything with world.add>
Win / Lose: <success and, if any, failure>

Then EXACTLY ONE fenced ```{fence} block with the complete {artifact}. Nothing after it.
