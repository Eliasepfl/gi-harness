# COMPOSITION_GAP — structural complexity vs the reference games, and whether the orchestrator can close it

Date: 2026-07-16. Synthesis of four verified lanes (skills-audit, reference-decomposition,
lit-composition, our-levers), each adversarially skeptic-checked against primary sources.
All numbers below are the SKEPTIC-CORRECTED values, not the first-pass lane figures.

Framing (Elias, binding): complexity ONLY, not polish. "Scene nodes" conflates
gameplay-structural complexity (rooms, cover, opponent logic, interacting mechanics) with
cosmetic nodes (meshes, materials, lights). Constraint: our artifact stays ONE self-contained
.gd (hermetic, `load()`/`preload()` banned) — composition happens IN CODE via helper functions,
never in .tscn files. Anti-anchoring law: no hardcoded taxonomies/examples/values on LLM
surfaces; principles and LLM-routed menus only.

---

## 1. The complexity-only gap, quantified (polish excluded)

Decomposition of the reference games' authored nodes (grep `^[node` on all non-addon .tscn;
every game-logic .gd read; all counts skeptic-recounted):

| Game | Total nodes | Gameplay-structural | RL sensors | Training replicas | Cosmetic |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FPS | 228 | ~93 (41%) | 72 RayCast3D | 0 | ~62 |
| 3DCarParking | 113 | ~35 (31%) | 17 | 32 GameScene copies | ~22 |
| ScoreTheGoal | 55 | ~25 (45%) | 4 | 8 playing_area copies | ~15 |

Only **31-45%** of authored nodes are complexity-bearing. Raw node counts (the Atlas ghost
table's 55-187 `n_nodes`) overstate the gap **~2-3x** (2.2x StG, 2.5x FPS, 3.2x CarParking):
they bundle sensor grids our `state()` JSON replaces, parallel-training replication `env.py`
makes unnecessary, and cosmetics that are out of scope. `ghosts.py` `n_nodes` counts every
`[node ...]` line including render nodes — it is a content-density proxy, not a complexity
metric, and must not be used as a target.

Our games (fly 413 / maze 254 / platformer 344 lines) carry 14-24 structural runtime nodes and
5 mechanics systems each, ~180-290 core-logic lines after subtracting GameAPI plumbing.
References externalize their plumbing into the godot_rl_agents addon + AIController subclasses;
excluding those, reference logic is StG 181 and CarParking 327 lines.

**Verdict on distance:**
- **vs ScoreTheGoal/CarParking class: NEAR PARITY.** Structural elements 14-24 vs 25-35;
  mechanics systems 5 vs 3-4; our spring-damper platforms and analytic ordered ring-threading
  are mechanically richer than anything in ScoreTheGoal.
- **vs FPS class: the real gap, and it is SPECIFIC, not diffuse:**
  1. **Arena spatial structure** — 54 hand-placed cover/wall/ramp meshes (16 SmallWall, 14 Box,
     5 Ramp, 4 Wall, 4 BigBox, 10 generic, 1 Ground) forming a deathmatch space vs our 6-14
     boxes forming rectangular shells: **~5x, the single largest deficit.** Note FPS's own
     collision is runtime-generated by 10-line Objects.gd (`create_trimesh_collision()` over
     children) — the reference itself is data-table → primitives, our exact helper pattern;
     only the 54-entry placement table lives in the editor instead of in code.
  2. **The combat interaction loop** — projectile+hitbox+health+respawn, ~112 lines + ~8
     functional nodes in FPS, absent in ours.
  3. **Per-episode randomization** — scoped correctly: CarParking re-randomizes fully each
     episode (spot shuffle + jitter), ScoreTheGoal partially (ball only; its goal-category
     randomization is dead code), and **FPS is layout-static like ours**. Our `_rng` is seeded
     but almost unused (platformer ±3px; fly/maze never draw).
  NOT more mechanics per se — FPS has ~6-7 systems vs our 5.

## 2. Verdict on Elias's hypothesis: can orchestrator + skills close it?

**Qualified YES — the knowledge for 2 of the 3 gap components is present, code-first, and
actually routed today; the third (spatial encounter design) is a genuine library hole.**
Three-way split over the 96-skill library (95 actually routable, see defects):

1. **THERE and ROUTED.** Opponent logic (genre-stealth, 437 lines, 0 .tscn: detection meters,
   vision cones, 4-state alert FSM, cover-by-raycast), procedural room layout
   (procedural-generation: BSP/walker/WFC/graph dungeons, 0 .tscn), pathing
   (navigation-pathfinding incl. RID-level NavigationServer bypassing nodes entirely), and
   encounter pacing (game-loop-waves) are ~zero-.tscn, hermetic-compatible, and the production
   LLM router delivers them: live probes returned 8-10 structural skills per rich prompt
   (FPS-hunt → stealth + shooter-fps + procgen + navigation + state-machine + 3d-world-building),
   reproduced independently by the skeptic. godot-master's matrix (Workflow 2 entity
   orchestration, Workflow 7 ProcGen→World→Navigation) is always injected unconditionally.
2. **THERE but mechanism-mismatched.** Scene streaming (metroidvania), MeshLibrary/CSG editor
   bakes (3d-world-building), @export Inspector wiring: principles transfer, mechanisms violate
   the hermetic ban, and no skill shows the in-code translation. The model bridges unaided.
3. **ABSENT.** Spatial encounter design as a discipline — cover placement, sightlines, arena
   flow, section-to-section difficulty — has NO dedicated skill; ~60-70 scattered lines
   (stealth §Level Design + platformer + metroidvania) is all there is. **This, not engine
   mechanics, is the knowledge gap behind the FPS-class deficit.** Per the anti-anchoring law,
   any fix is principles/menus, never a taxonomy.

Two skeptic REFUTATIONS that reshape the picture:
- **Complexity-vs-polish does NOT map onto code-vs-editor.** The .tscn-heavy skills are project
  PLUMBING (templates, scene-management, CI/export), while the polish skills (3d-lighting,
  3d-materials, particles) are also ~zero-.tscn. The real hermetic incompatibility is ASSET
  dependence (3d-materials has 15 `load("res://textures/...")` calls) — a different axis. The
  one-file constraint filters out plumbing, not polish; polish must be excluded by MEASUREMENT
  choice, not by the constraint.
- **The measurement channel is NOT cosmetic-blind "by construction" — only by contract.**
  serve_game.gd derives geometry from the game's self-reported `state()["bodies"]`; no surface
  walks the physics tree. A complexity descriptor on this channel is inflatable by
  state()-padding (footprint-less marker bodies). Any complexity metric needs an anti-gaming
  story (footprint-carrying bodies only, or a host-side tree-walk cross-check) before it can
  anchor a gate.

Routing caveats (all verified): the LLM router's 10-pick ceiling BINDS on rich prompts (real
routing is truncated); `_llm_route` failure silently degrades production to BM25/no-context;
`_injected_skills` makes a SECOND independent routing call for the ledger, so logged skills can
diverge from injected skills. Library defects: skills_index.json lists
"godot-navigation-pathfinding" twice and "godot-ai-navigation" not at all (that skill is dead
weight, 95/96 routable, and the duplicate deflates BM25 IDF for navigation terms);
godot-composition's index advertises "Level Design" with zero level-design body content (BM25
retrieval trap).

Literature backing (all claims verified to primary sources): an explicit intermediate
representation is load-bearing (Parsel arXiv:2212.10561 — direct plan→code ablation drops
25.5%→6%; caveat: Parsel's mechanism is IR + per-function TEST SEARCH, so a spec stage without
checkable per-helper acceptance imports half the mechanism); plan-then-implement gives up to
+25.4% relative pass@1 (arXiv:2303.06689); one-shot generation plateaus around ~257-LOC
single-file games even for frontier models (V-GameGym arXiv:2509.20136 — best solve count o3
1092/2219 ≈49%; the "45.0" is a composite score, not a success rate); dependency-complex,
multi-turn reuse degrades — keep helper graphs SHALLOW (spec → builders → integration,
CodeFlowBench arXiv:2504.21751); multi-candidate generate+SELECT beats one-shot, but rich
feedback goes unused (AVR-Agent arXiv:2508.00632) — iterate-and-select, not freeform iteration.
"LLM writes a grammar → deterministic expander" is a plausible engineering bet, NOT an
established pipeline (the LLM→constraints line is young, non-game, 17-70% reliability).
Retrieval demonstrably changes which constructs get called (DocPrompting arXiv:2207.05987) and
plausibly matters more for low-resource GDScript (CodeRAG-Bench arXiv:2406.14497), but NO paper
measures retrieval's effect on structural complexity — "skills increase structure" is a testable
hypothesis with a plausible mechanism, and the harness must generate its own evidence. (Side
note: JAMER/JamBench, arXiv:2606.19830, is an emerging headless-Godot benchmark worth reading.)

**Bottom line:** the orchestrator + skills can close the opponent-logic, procgen-layout, and
pacing components of the gap with what is injected today. The arena/encounter-design component
needs (i) the missing principle-level knowledge and (ii) measurement pressure — nothing in the
funnel currently measures structure, so prompt-side intent alone is unenforceable (repo
precedent: 3/3 3D-evoking prompts produced 2D despite a compliant "Dimension is YOURS"
paragraph, DEAD_SPACE_GATE.md).

## 3. Ranked lever plan (anti-anchoring-compliant only)

Compliance bar, by repo precedent: aspect-level names allowed, instance-level lists banned
(STAKES section = canonical). Governance precedent (DEAD_SPACE_GATE.md): an equally compliant
prompt lever (Dimension) was REVERTED pending Elias's call — prompt-surface levers (L3) need
his explicit sign-off before shipping.

**L1 — Complexity descriptors (harness-side measurement; do first).**
Add to descriptors.py: static-geometry partition count (connected static-body clusters from the
t=0 facts dead-space already uses), interaction density (distinct body pairs contacting during
the witness replay), gating depth (checkpoints latching in strict sequence), autonomous-body
count (non-controlled bodies whose velocity changes without contact/player action). All
content-free, computed from existing artifacts, and cosmetic-blind by measurement choice.
Effort: small for axes + the REQUIRED anti-gaming guard (count footprint-carrying bodies only,
or add a host-side tree-walk cross-check vs the state() report). Effect: creates the missing
measurement that makes every other lever enforceable, and gives the ghost comparison a
complexity axis that is not `n_nodes`. Risk: Goodhart via state()-padding if the guard is
skipped; avoid code-shape metrics (helper counts) as primary axes — they are the gameable ones.

**L2 — Router aspect widening (one phrase).**
Add "its world structure / level layout" to the `_llm_route` aspect list
(skill_context.py:327-329). This IS the LLM-routed-menu pattern the law prescribes. Skeptic
upgraded this lever: 2 of the 3 target skills are already code-centric and hermetic-compatible
(godot-composition: 0 preload/0 .tscn, signal/component architecture; procedural-generation:
0 preload, seeded runtime generation) — cheaper and lower-risk than first assessed. Effect is a
bias shift on the primary path (the router reads full descriptions), not an unlock. Risk
concentrated in godot-3d-world-building (MeshLibrary/preload) and threaded chunk-gen scripts;
monitor `pipeline.skills` vs G0 ENV_ERROR rates in the ledger (already recorded; fix the
`_injected_skills` double-call first so logs match injections). Also fix: the duplicate
navigation index entry; godot-composition's bogus "Level Design" keyword.

**L3 — Composition principle + `Structure:` DESIGN field (needs Elias sign-off).**
STAKES-style section in api_gdscript.md ("the world is made of PARTS — sections with a role and
a mechanic that lives there") at structure-altitude, zero instances, plus one
`Structure: <the named parts and which mechanic lives in each>` line in the DESIGN block.
Effort: trivial. Effect: real but shallow ALONE — unverified fields fill with fluff; the repo's
proven pattern is field+gate pairs (Pressure + failure-witness). Ship only with L4.

**L4 — "Too simple" advisory gate (field+gate closure for L3).**
Dead-space template: measured fact (partition count == 1 AND minimal interaction density) →
principle-phrased directive ("the world is a single enclosing boundary with unstructured
contents — give it parts or couple its systems, keeping the goal reachable") → advisory,
DIFFICULTY-tier, never blocks. Arbitration rule: never compile the too-simple row in a round
that carries any G3' difficulty row OR a dead_space row (both push the opposite direction on
the same facts; skeptic showed the dead_space collision is the more direct one). Worst case is
a wasted nudge round, never a lost certification — G3' terminates HARDENED_HARD (a success
verdict) and never revokes cert.

**L5 — QD pressure toward complex Atlas cells.**
Only after L1 axes exist and correlate with anything. Complexity cells count only when
CERTIFIED, so solver-unreachable complexity dies at G3 — the bound is real but leaks through
the state()-padding hole until L1's guard exists. New machinery (curriculum.py chassis; Atlas
is read-only D1). Effort: medium-large. Sequence last among measurement levers.

**L6 — Two-turn outline→code generation (hold).**
Lit-supported (Parsel/self-planning) but marginal for reasoning models that already deliberate
privately (~14.5k thinking tokens); the strong case is the reasoning-off salvage path where an
outline turn is deliberation a provider cannot eat, plus an inspectable structural commitment
to correlate with L1 descriptors. If adopted, mandate something CHECKABLE per helper (Parsel's
other half). Adopt only if L1-L4 plateau.

### The concrete complexity milestone: FPS-with-scripted-opponent

**Feasible now, in-envelope.** Verified arithmetic: FPS's full combat stack is ~112 lines
(PlayerHitBox 4 + health_system 23 + projectile 48 + shoot/hit ~37) + ~8 functional nodes; our
maze already ships a patrolling opponent with catch-reset in ~17 behavior lines. A patrolling,
shootable, respawning enemy in hermetic one-.gd style ≈ 150-200 lines, ~10 runtime nodes — well
inside the existing 254-413 line envelope. (FPS itself has ZERO scripted AI — its opponent is a
second RL agent; a scripted guard is strictly OUR addition, and genre-stealth carries exactly
the needed FSM/patrol/vision knowledge, already routed on FPS-shaped prompts.)
**What's missing is the arena, not the enemy:** a ~54-entry placement table (or a seeded
generator — BSP/walker from procedural-generation) feeding a box/prism emitter, the same
data-table pattern fly's RING_DATA already uses in miniature. The authoring effort does not
disappear — the reference hand-placed those 54 transforms — which is precisely why the
encounter-design knowledge hole (§2.3) and the L1 measurement matter: they are what push a
generator beyond rectangular shells. Plus per-episode randomization from the already-seeded
`_rng` (CarParking is the exemplar; note FPS itself is layout-static, so on this axis we'd
EXCEED the reference).

## 4. What NOT to do (explicitly deprioritized)

- **No dresser/polish investment.** Cosmetic runtime nodes (meshes, materials, lights, fog),
  3d-lighting/3d-materials/particles skills, visual richness — Elias excluded these. The
  asset-dependent skills (15 `load()` texture calls in 3d-materials) are hermetically
  incompatible anyway.
- **No `n_nodes` targets.** The ghost-table node count is 2-3x-inflated by sensors, replication,
  and cosmetics; matching it would optimize the conflation Elias rejected.
- **No .tscn-mechanism ports.** Scene-management transitions, ResourceLoader room streaming,
  MeshLibrary/CSG editor bakes, MCP scene-builder tooling — plumbing incompatible with the
  hermetic lane; principles transfer via L2's code-centric skills instead.
- **No few-shot exemplar games and no content taxonomies on any LLM surface** — the
  best-documented structure-shaping lever in the literature (exemplars) is exactly what the
  anti-anchoring law bans; the compliant substitutes are the L1-L4 process/representation levers.
- **No code-shape complexity metrics as primary axes** (helper-function counts, line counts) —
  gameable by renaming, the cosmetic-decomposition failure mode.
- **No long freeform iteration loops** — evidence supports staged decomposition + select, not
  feedback-marination (AVR-Agent's own negative result; CodeFlowBench degradation).

## 5. First measurable experiment

**Question:** does router aspect-widening (L2, the top no-sign-off generation lever) shift
structural complexity without hurting certification? L1 descriptors are the judge, not the
lever, so they land first.

Design:
1. Prereqs (order): implement L1 axes (partition count, interaction density, gating depth,
   autonomous-body count) with the footprint-only guard; fix the `_injected_skills`
   double-routing so the ledger records what was actually injected; fix the duplicate
   navigation index entry.
2. Arms: A = current aspect list; B = aspect list + "its world structure / level layout".
   Identical model, seeds, prompt set. Prompts: ~24 per arm, drawn structurally-agnostic (the
   normal prompt source, NOT hand-written composition prompts — that would anchor the test),
   plus a tagged 6-prompt FPS-milestone family ("arena + patrolling opponent" shaped) analyzed
   separately.
3. Metrics per arm: (i) the four L1 descriptors on certified games; (ii) certification rate;
   (iii) G0 ENV_ERROR / banned-API rate and repair-attempt count (the L2 risk channel);
   (iv) injected-skill sets from the fixed ledger (manipulation check: did structure skills
   actually inject more often in B?).
4. Success: B shifts ≥2 of 4 structural descriptors on certified games with certification-rate
   drop <5 points and no G0 ENV_ERROR blow-up. Failure with a positive manipulation check =
   knowledge injected but unused → escalates priority of L3+L4 (intent+gate) and the milestone
   experiment. Failure WITH a failed manipulation check = router problem, iterate on L2 phrasing.
5. Milestone family read-out: autonomous-body count ≥1 + interaction density above the
   descriptor median + certified = first FPS-class structural game; any certified instance is
   the go signal for making the milestone a standing Atlas target (L5).

Caveat from the literature lane, kept verbatim in spirit: nothing in the cited work predicts
effect sizes here — this experiment IS the evidence-generation step; n≈24/arm bounds noise on
cert-rate at roughly ±10 points, so treat descriptor shifts, not cert deltas, as the primary
endpoint at this n.
